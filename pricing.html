Frequently asked questions

Can I use HelloEarth without paying?

Yes. The Free plan lets you keep up to three students and up to ten new lesson notes per month, with a maximum of thirty saved notes.

Can I upgrade or pay today?

Not yet. We're preparing secure card subscriptions. Paid prices are shown for planning only. Contact HelloEarth to join the launch list.

Will I lose student data if I reach my limit?

No. Existing records stay in your private account. Once a limit is reached, new inserts are blocked until more capacity is available. We won't automatically delete notes to make space.

Can I run Atlas AI on my own computer?

Yes. Atlas AI is being developed as a standalone local application that uses Ollama for AI inference. It is currently a separate prototype, not a feature of the website's student dashboard. Synced student records and sign-in still use Supabase.

Can I connect my own OpenAI or another AI provider API?

Bring-your-own AI API connections are planned, not available yet. When implemented, you would pay your chosen provider directly for usage. Atlas will need secure local credential handling; never paste an API secret into a public webpage or student note.

Which plans will offer cloud-hosted Atlas?

We are exploring a managed Atlas option for higher-tier Growth and Studio subscribers. Growth would be an optional usage-priced add-on; Studio may have a capped allowance. Exact limits, prices and availability will be published before customers can subscribe. Neither option is live yet.

Are recordings or unlimited AI generation included?

No. Recordings and website AI generation are not currently provided. Managed AI usage will have hard limits and clear pricing rather than an unlimited promise.
