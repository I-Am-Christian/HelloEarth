Frequently asked questions

Can I use HelloEarth without paying?

Yes. The Free plan lets you keep up to three students and up to ten new lesson notes per month, with a maximum of thirty saved notes.

Can I upgrade or pay today?

Not yet. We're preparing secure card subscriptions. Paid prices are shown for planning only. Contact HelloEarth to join the launch list.

Will I lose student data if I reach my limit?

No. Existing records stay in your private account. Once a limit is reached, new inserts are blocked until more capacity is available. We won't automatically delete notes to make space.

Are recordings and AI lessons included?

No. At this stage, HelloEarth is a text-first student and lesson-notes workspace. Cloud AI credits, recordings, invoicing and other future features would require separately published limits and pricing.
