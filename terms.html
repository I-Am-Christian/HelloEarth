Terms & Conditions

Terms for using the HelloEarth early-access tutoring workspace.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. About HelloEarth

HelloEarth is a public website and early-access tutor workspace operated by Little Universe, a business registered in South Africa. Tutors can create accounts and manage student learning profiles and lesson notes. Atlas AI, transcription and some illustrated dashboard previews describe features in development, not presently available services.

2. Accounts and security

You must use accurate account information, keep passwords and authentication credentials private, and only access accounts you own or are authorised to use. You are responsible for activity under your account to the extent permitted by law.

3. Student data

Only enter student information you have a lawful basis and appropriate permissions to store. Tutors are responsible for informing their students about the use of HelloEarth and obtaining required consent, including parent or guardian permission where applicable to children. Do not enter highly sensitive personal information, recordings or documents in this early-access version.

4. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to access other tutors' information, attack our systems, bypass access controls, upload unlawful content or misuse the service. We may restrict access to protect users and the platform.

5. Early-access availability

The service is actively evolving and can change, experience downtime or lose features. Please keep independent backups of important teaching records. Features marked coming soon, including Atlas AI integration, are not guaranteed to launch on any particular date.

6. Third-party services

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Supabase for authentication and database services. Optional Google sign-in and Google Calendar integration involve Google services. Calendar events are created only when you submit a scheduling request with authorized editing access. Verify appointment details before saving. Third-party service terms and privacy terms may also apply.

7. Content and intellectual property

You retain rights to original content you enter, subject to the permission needed for HelloEarth to process and display it for you. HelloEarth's original branding and site design remain protected as allowed by law.

8. Leaderboard, rewards and service fee preview

The leaderboard shows voluntary public tutor aliases and aggregated activity recorded within the service. Reported lessons, attendance and booking requests may be self-reported and are not guaranteed to be independently verified. Joining is optional and can be reversed from the dashboard. Falsifying records to manipulate rankings or obtain benefits is prohibited. Badges are non-financial acknowledgements.

Hello Earth's displayed 20% to 12% fee progression over 1,000 platform-verified paid lessons is a proposed future platform service fee, not a legal tax reduction. It is a preview only: payment processing, fee collection and the associated financial reward program are not currently active. Manual lesson logs do not count as payment verification, and no financial discount or payout is promised. If paid lessons launch, a separate applicable agreement and verified payment records will define the final terms. Tutors remain responsible for their own tax obligations under applicable law.

9. Limitation and legal protections

We seek to operate the service responsibly, but do not promise uninterrupted availability or that it will meet every professional requirement. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law, including consumer and privacy protections.

10. Contact and updates

Terms may be updated as the platform evolves. Contact helloearthsupport@gmail.com with account or support requests. These terms are intended to be interpreted under South African law, subject to mandatory protections.
