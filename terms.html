Terms & Conditions

Terms for using Hello Earth's early-access student records database.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. About HelloEarth

Hello Earth is an early-access student records database operated by Little Universe, a business registered in South Africa. Current workspaces are private to individual educator accounts and may be used to organise learner profiles, assessments and notes. Shared school databases, staff roles, group/class records and institution-wide reporting are planned, not currently available. Some illustrated features, including Atlas AI, remain in development.

2. Accounts and security

You must use accurate account information, keep passwords and authentication credentials private, and only access accounts you own or are authorised to use. You are responsible for activity under your account to the extent permitted by law.

3. Student data

Only enter student information you have a lawful basis and appropriate permissions to store. Educators are responsible for informing learners and their families about the use of HelloEarth and obtaining required consent, including parent or guardian permission where applicable to children. Do not enter highly sensitive personal information, recordings or documents in this early-access version.

4. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to access other account holders' information, attack our systems, bypass access controls, upload unlawful content or misuse the service. We may restrict access to protect users and the platform.

5. Early-access availability

The service is actively evolving and can change, experience downtime or lose features. Please keep independent backups of important student records. Features marked coming soon, including Atlas AI integration, are not guaranteed to launch on any particular date.

6. Third-party services

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Supabase for authentication and database services. Optional Google sign-in and Google Calendar integration involve Google services. Calendar events are created only when you submit a scheduling request with authorized editing access. Verify appointment details before saving. Third-party service terms and privacy terms may also apply.

7. Content and intellectual property

You retain rights to original content you enter, subject to the permission needed for HelloEarth to process and display it for you. HelloEarth's original branding and site design remain protected as allowed by law.

8. Product scope and discontinued concepts

Hello Earth is not a tutoring marketplace and does not broker or charge commission on private lessons. The public tutor leaderboard and proposed lesson-fee reward scheme have been discontinued. Previous account records remain subject to the retention and privacy arrangements in our Privacy Policy.

Shared school and learning-centre workspaces will not be offered as a production service until appropriate organisation-level access controls, staff roles, data protection and account agreements are available. Individual users must not share their private account credentials as a substitute for staff permissions.

9. Limitation and legal protections

We seek to operate the service responsibly, but do not promise uninterrupted availability or that it will meet every professional requirement. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law, including consumer and privacy protections.

10. Contact and updates

Terms may be updated as the platform evolves. Contact helloearthsupport@gmail.com with account or support requests. These terms are intended to be interpreted under South African law, subject to mandatory protections.
