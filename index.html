Looking ahead

Meet Atlas AI.

Atlas AI is our standalone, local-first AI assistant. It runs on your own computer using Ollama; student records can sync separately through your signed-in HelloEarth account. Direct website integration is still in development.

Local Atlas prototype · website integration coming later
✦ Memory-aware assistance✦ Lesson-planning ideas✦ Student insights
Atlas is currently a separate local prototype. AI API connections and managed cloud access are future options.
What we're exploring

Thoughtful assistance for the way tutors teach.

Our plan is simple: Free and Starter run Atlas locally on your computer; Growth and Studio use a managed cloud version. Every tier will also have the choice to connect an external AI API. The local Atlas prototype exists separately today; web AI access, external API integrations and hosted Atlas are planned.

✦

Lesson planning support

Ideas for activities, vocabulary practice and follow-up lessons informed by a learner's goals and language level.

◎

Clearer progress insights

Spot recurring strengths, areas to practise and possible next steps, with the tutor reviewing every suggestion.

One Atlas, multiple ways to run it

Local for Free and Starter, managed cloud for Growth and Studio, and bring-your-own AI API on every tier. Cloud access will have clear monthly limits; student context requires appropriate consent. The integrations are still in development.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

The essentials, without the fine print.

Is HelloEarth available now?

Yes, the free student-management workspace is available now. Paid plans, website AI features and hosted Atlas remain in development.

Who is it for?

Independent tutors who want one place to organize learners, lesson notes and progress.

Does Atlas AI work here already?

Atlas AI currently runs as a separate local prototype using Ollama. Our planned tiers are local Atlas on Free and Starter, cloud Atlas on Growth and Studio, with the option to connect your own supported AI API on every plan. These integrations are still in development.
