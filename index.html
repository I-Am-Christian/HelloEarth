WHAT'S NEXT

Atlas AI is in development.

A separate local Atlas prototype already runs on a computer using Ollama, but AI is not integrated into the HelloEarth dashboard yet. Planned features include AI-assisted lesson ideas, optional transcription and support for your own compatible AI API.

Future: local AI on entry tiers · cloud AI on higher tiers

The current dashboard uses editable lesson templates, not AI-generated lessons.

Your teaching data stays private.

Student records are stored in your authenticated Supabase workspace. Google Calendar access and push notifications are optional.
