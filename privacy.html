Privacy Policy

How your account and private teaching records are handled in the HelloEarth early-access workspace.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. What HelloEarth offers

HelloEarth provides a public website and an early-access, account-based tutor workspace. Registered tutors can organise learning profiles and lesson notes. The Atlas AI assistant and transcription features remain in development and are not active in the public workspace.

2. Information we process

For account registration and access, our authentication provider processes your name (if supplied), email address, password credentials or Google sign-in information, authentication identifiers and session data. Passwords are handled through Supabase Auth rather than stored in the HelloEarth website's code. Your tutor account may store student display names, CEFR levels, learning goals, interests, lesson notes, assessments and other information you choose to add.

3. Why we use it

We use account information to authenticate you and provide the workspace, and tutor-submitted student information to display and manage your private teaching records. We do not currently train an AI model on the contents of the public tutor workspace.

4. Storage, location and access

Our static website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Account and student records are stored with Supabase in an EU-region database. Tutor accounts are protected by Supabase authentication and row-level security rules designed to isolate records by tutor ID. Access and security depend on proper account use and service configuration; please use a unique password and keep sign-in details private.

5. Cookies, sessions and technical information

Authentication uses browser session storage mechanisms to keep you signed in. The website does not currently use advertising trackers. Our hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, device and security logs to deliver services and protect accounts.

6. Google sign-in and external providers

If you choose Google sign-in when available, Google's authentication service and Supabase Auth process information needed to establish your session. Google OAuth must be configured by HelloEarth before this option will work. You can instead create an account using email and password.

7. Student information and minors

Tutors must only enter student information they are permitted to collect and share with this service, with appropriate notices and consent as required. For children, ensure appropriate parent or guardian permission and avoid recording unnecessary sensitive information. Do not upload lesson recordings, medical data or identity documents into the current student workspace.

8. Retention and deletion

Account information and student entries remain until they are deleted or until HelloEarth changes its service and retention arrangements, subject to legal obligations. The current early-access dashboard does not yet include self-service account deletion or full data export. To request access, correction, export or deletion, email helloearthsupport@gmail.com. Backups and provider logs may have their own retention periods.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable laws, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may have rights of access, correction, objection and deletion. Contact us for requests concerning the information we control.

10. Changes and contact

As the platform develops we may update this policy. For privacy enquiries, contact helloearthsupport@gmail.com.
